隐私政策 · 天元雀霸

生效日期：2026-10-31（单机公测版）

适用范围

本政策适用于「天元雀霸」（包名 net.mahjongx.queba）Mac 与 iPhone 单机公测版（通过 TestFlight 分发）。

开发者

天元雀霸开发团队

我们不收集什么

本版本无需注册，游戏运行时不联网，不收集、不向开发者传输任何个人数据，不追踪用户，不含广告与第三方统计工具。游戏进度和设置只保存在您的设备本机，不提供云存档。

网页链接

游戏「设置 → 关于」里有一个「阅读原作小说《天元·雀霸传奇》」按钮。只有您主动点击，才会用系统浏览器打开小说官网 novel.mahjongx.net；该网站的数据处理见其隐私政策：https://novel.mahjongx.net/privacy.html 。

TestFlight

通过 TestFlight 安装时，Apple 会按其隐私政策处理测试员的账号信息、崩溃与使用反馈，这不属于游戏向我们传输数据。

删除数据

在 iPhone 上，删除 App 即删除本机的游戏进度与设置。在 Mac 上，删除 App 后数据仍留在系统为 App 保留的文件夹中，如需一并删除，请删除个人「资源库」里的「Containers/net.mahjongx.queba」文件夹（路径 ~/Library/Containers/net.mahjongx.queba）。我们无法恢复已删除的进度。

儿童

本版本不向包括儿童在内的任何用户收集个人数据，也不要求提供身份或联系方式。

游戏内虚拟物品

游戏内的金币、雀玉只用于游戏内外观和玩法，不能购买，也不能兑换现金或实物、不可转让。

联系我们

如有隐私问题，请写信到 support@mahjongx.net。您主动发来的邮件内容仅用于处理您的问题，请勿在邮件中提供密码等敏感信息。

后续版本

以后若开放账号、云存档、好友房等联网功能，会先在本页发布新的隐私政策，并在 App 内告知。公测进度能否迁移至正式版尚未确定，不作承诺。

Privacy Policy · 天元雀霸

Effective: 2026-10-31 (offline public beta)

Scope

This policy covers the offline public beta of 天元雀霸 (bundle ID net.mahjongx.queba) for Mac and iPhone, distributed through TestFlight.

Developer

The Tianyuan Queba Team

What we do not collect

This version needs no registration and makes no network connections while you play. It does not collect or send any personal data to the developer, does not track you, and contains no ads or third-party analytics. Progress and settings stay on your device; there are no cloud saves.

Web link

Under Settings → About there is a button, "Read the original novel Tianyuan: Legend of the Mahjong King". Only if you tap it does your system browser open the novel site novel.mahjongx.net, whose data handling is described in its own policy: https://novel.mahjongx.net/privacy.html .

TestFlight

When installed via TestFlight, Apple processes tester account information, crash reports and feedback under Apple's privacy policy. This is not data sent by the game to us.

Deleting data

On iPhone, deleting the app deletes its local progress and settings. On Mac, after you delete the app its data remains in the folder the system keeps for it; to delete that too, remove the folder "Containers/net.mahjongx.queba" in your user Library (~/Library/Containers/net.mahjongx.queba). We cannot restore deleted progress.

Children

This version collects no personal data from anyone, including children, and asks for no identity or contact details.

In-game items

In-game Gold coins and Mahjong Jade are used only inside the game; they cannot be purchased, cashed out, exchanged for goods, or transferred.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@mahjongx.net. Anything you send is used only to handle your question; please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Future versions

If we later add online features such as accounts, cloud saves or friend rooms, we will first publish a new privacy policy on this page and notify you in the app. Whether beta progress can be transferred to the full release is undecided and not promised.
