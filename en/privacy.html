Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The official site of Legend of the Mahjong King (novel.mahjongx.net) and the 天元·雀霸傳奇 Facebook Page are run by the author of the novel. We collect as little as we can.

What we collect

Visit statistics: when you open a page we record the time, the page address, the referring site, your approximate country and city, your device type (mobile or desktop), the promotion tag of the link you came through (for example a Facebook tag in the address), and a random identifier created in your browser; we also note how long you stay on the page and how far down you read. Your IP address is stored only as a salted hash, never in raw form. This is used only to count readers and reading progress.

Guestbook: the nickname and message you choose to submit, together with the message language, your hashed IP and approximate country (to prevent spam). Messages are published only after the author reviews them.

Referral program: when you create a referral link we store its code, a private key for checking progress, and your hashed IP. When someone arrives through your link we record, by hashed IP, when they first visited, their approximate country and whether they read a chapter, so we can count them. When rewards are later redeemed in the game Queba Mahjong, the game asks us for this count using the private key.

Accounts: playing the game Queba Mahjong requires an account, and the official site will also offer sign-up (one account for both). When you register and play, we collect the account name or nickname you choose, your email address or phone number (for signing in, account recovery and verification codes), your password (stored only in encrypted form), and records tied to your account such as game progress, match history and referral rewards. These are used only to let you sign in, keep your progress, deliver rewards and keep your account secure. The exact fields are shown on the sign-up page, and we will update this policy when sign-up goes live.

Settings kept in your browser: the site stores the random identifier, promotion tag and referral code mentioned above in your browser, along with your reading settings (text size, font, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, language, night mode). Reading settings stay in your browser and are never uploaded. This site does not use cookies.

What we do not do

・Reading the novel on this site does not require an account; if you do not register, we do not collect names, phone numbers or email addresses.

・We do not collect card or payment details. If the game later offers purchases, payment is handled by the app store or payment provider and we do not store card numbers.

・We never sell or rent any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

・The "QuebaLegend Poster" app is used only to publish excerpts of the novel on our own Facebook Page. It does not read or store personal data of any Facebook user.

Where data is kept

Statistics, guestbook messages and referral records are stored on Cloudflare; account and game data are stored on the game's servers. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which receive your request under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

To access, correct or delete your guestbook messages, referral records or account data (including closing your account), email media@mahjongx.net. We will act within 30 days.
