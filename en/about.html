About this work

Legend of the Mahjong King is an original fantasy novel by Master of the Rain Pavilion, set in the world of the Mahjong Way. The story spans five volumes and approximately forty chapters. This website, novel.mahjongx.net, is the sole first-publication venue for the work.

Publication schedule: Volume I updates daily at 20:00 (UTC+8). After Volume I, new chapters appear every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 20:00 (UTC+8).

The characters will also appear in the companion competitive mahjong strategy game, Mahjong King.

This website collects anonymous visit statistics only: the time of each visit, the page visited, how long the page was read and how far down it, the country or region, and the device type. Your browser keeps a random visitor number (containing no personal information) so that repeat visits by the same reader count as one. IP addresses are stored only in an irreversibly encrypted form to count visitors. They are not used for any other purpose or shared with any third party.

About AI assistance

The author conceived the world, characters and course of the story, makes the creative decisions for each chapter, and reviews and approves every chapter. AI assists with the prose, which is revised repeatedly in accordance with the author’s feedback.

We disclose this openly and retain the complete creative record.

This translation was made from the Chinese original with AI assistance. Where the two differ, the Chinese original prevails.
