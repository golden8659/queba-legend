About this work

Legend of the Mahjong King is an original fantasy novel by Master of the Rain Pavilion, set in the world of the Mahjong Way. Part One spans five volumes and forty chapters, and is complete. The work was first published on this website, novel.mahjongx.net.

Publication schedule: new chapters appear every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 8 p.m. Singapore time (SGT).

The characters will also appear in the companion competitive mahjong strategy game, Queba Mahjong.

About AI assistance

The author conceived the world, characters and course of the story, makes the creative decisions for each chapter, and reviews and approves every chapter. AI assists with the prose, which is revised repeatedly in accordance with the author’s feedback.
